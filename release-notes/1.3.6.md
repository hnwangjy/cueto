# Cueto 1.3.6

音乐播放与暂停采用定向控制，并根据实际播放状态同步按钮显示，提升多应用使用场景下的控制可靠性。菜单栏应用图标保留原有色彩，图标与名称的垂直对齐同步优化。

Music play and pause commands now target the Music app directly, with button states confirmed by the app. Menu bar app icons retain their original colors, with refined vertical alignment for icons and names.
