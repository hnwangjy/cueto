# Cueto 1.3.1

可在「设置 → 实验功能」开启 Apple Music 同步歌词。开启自动检查更新后，Cueto 会在启动时后台检查。手动检查的窗口会显示在设置前方，无音频时菜单栏会完整显示 Cueto。

Enable synced Apple Music lyrics in Settings under Experimental Features. When automatic update checks are enabled, Cueto checks on launch. Manual update dialogs appear in front of Settings, and the idle menu bar item has room for the full Cueto name.
