# Cueto 1.3.4

歌词与字幕采用更紧凑的固定栏位，换句统一轻淡入。长句先显示句首再缓慢滚动；播放中间的空档保留上一句片刻后留白，不再跳回歌名。新增实验性小宇宙异常恢复，可在设置中开启，仅在报告确认已知异常后重新打开应用。

Lyrics and captions now use a more compact fixed-width area with consistent fades. Long lines pause at the beginning before scrolling. Mid-track gaps briefly retain the previous line, then stay blank. Experimental Xiaoyuzhou recovery can reopen the app after a diagnostic report confirms the known termination issue.
