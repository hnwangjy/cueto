# Cueto 1.3.8

播放进度，随手掌握。播放面板支持点击与拖动定位，拖动时同步预览已播放与剩余时间，松手后跳转，并保留播放或暂停状态。

进度条从封面提取代表色，根据明暗外观调整亮度与对比度。悬停与按下时，轨道平滑放大、提亮；无可用主色时回归白色。

Playback progress, within reach. Click or drag the progress bar to seek, with elapsed and remaining time previewed as you drag. Release to jump while preserving the current playback state.

The progress bar draws its color from the artwork, adjusting brightness and contrast for light and dark appearances. Hover and press bring a smooth expansion and brighter feedback. When no suitable artwork color is available, the bar returns to white.
