# Cueto 1.4.1

- 双指调节菜单栏音量时，每跨过 5% 提供一次触觉反馈，可在「设置 → 通用」中关闭。
- 触控板和鼠标向上滚动提高音量，向下滚动降低音量。
- 歌词固定显示在播放控制左侧，音量条展开时无遮挡。

- When you adjust menu bar volume with a two-finger gesture, the trackpad provides feedback at 5% intervals. Turn it off in Settings → General.
- Scroll up to raise the volume and down to lower it on a trackpad or mouse.
- Lyrics stay to the left of the playback controls, and the volume indicator stays clear of tooltips.
