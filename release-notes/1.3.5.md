# Cueto 1.3.5

音乐歌词与小宇宙字幕现在可以分别设置显示宽度和滚动速度。小宇宙字幕默认采用长宽度、慢速，每句开头先停留片刻再滚动。设置窗口采用更融合的磨砂布局，并收紧顶部与内容留白。

Music lyrics and Xiaoyuzhou captions now have separate width and scroll speed settings. Xiaoyuzhou defaults to a wide display and slow scrolling, with a brief pause at the beginning of each line. Settings has a unified frosted layout with tighter spacing.
