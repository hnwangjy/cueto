# Cueto 1.4.0

- 悬停菜单栏控制区时，音量浮层轻微放大并向下展开，移开指针后向上收回。
- 播放进度条采用更纤细的圆角轨道，悬停时平滑变粗，拖动圆点从当前进度展开。

- Hover over the menu bar controls to reveal the volume indicator with a subtle scale and downward motion. Move the pointer away to retract it upward.
- The playback progress bar has a slimmer rounded track that expands smoothly on hover, with the scrubber growing from the current position.
